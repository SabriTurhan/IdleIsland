# Yandex Games — store listing (Island Escape: Craft & Sail)

Language of the game and the listing: English (all in-game text is English; requirement 8.2.3).

## Title
Island Escape: Craft & Sail

## Short description
Survive two islands, build a camp, forge a sword and sail away on a pirate ship.

## Description
A storm has washed you ashore. Chop trees, mine stone and gather berries to survive, then turn your camp into a working base: craft planks and ropes, cook food, build a storage and rescue castaways who will gather resources for you.

When your raft is ready, a second storm throws you onto Grimbeard's cursed island. Burn the purple thorns with torches, fight skeleton pirates, restore the old forge and upgrade your sword. Find the four pieces of the treasure map, defeat the Ghost Captain in his crypt and repair his ship to escape.

- Two islands with their own story and more than 70 missions
- Gathering, crafting and building that keeps growing your base
- Helpers who work for you while you explore
- Sword upgrades, skeleton enemies and a boss fight
- Fishing, digging for treasure and upgrades for speed, capacity and tools

## How to play
Move with the on-screen joystick (drag anywhere on the screen) or with WASD / arrow keys on a computer. Your hero gathers, attacks and builds automatically: walk up to a tree, rock or enemy and stay next to it. Bring the required resources to a building site and stand on it to build. Follow the mission panel at the top of the screen: it shows what to do next and points the way.
